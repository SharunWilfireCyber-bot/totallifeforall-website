About us

A community-led approach to total life for all

Total Life for All Community Development Initiative (TL4A) is a non-profit organisation working to advance sustainable development and humanitarian outreach across Nigeria. We work alongside communities — not above them — to strengthen access to clean water, healthy environments, quality education and economic opportunity.

Our work is grounded in the belief that informed communities, inclusive leadership and meaningful partnerships are what turn short-term relief into lasting change.

Our focus areas

Programmes that meet real needs

Six interconnected areas of work, designed with communities and delivered through partnerships.

WASH

Water, sanitation and hygiene promotion — safe water access, hygiene education and sanitation facilities for underserved communities.

Climate Action & Environment

Tree planting, waste management awareness and climate resilience initiatives that protect livelihoods and local ecosystems.

Women's Empowerment

Skills training, economic empowerment and leadership development so women can lead change in their households and communities.

Youth & Agricultural Development

Practical agricultural training, entrepreneurship and mentorship that turn young people into producers and employers.

Education & Community Development

Literacy support, learning materials and community infrastructure that keep children in school and neighbourhoods strong.

Women's Rights & Leadership

Advocating for women's rights, participation, leadership and stronger voices in community and environmental decisions.
