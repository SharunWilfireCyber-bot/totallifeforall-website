Who We Are

Total Life for All Community Development Initiative

TL4A is committed to improving the quality of life of vulnerable and underserved populations through practical, community-focused development work.

We promote access to quality education, healthcare, protection of human rights and sustainable economic opportunities through advocacy, community-based interventions, capacity building, strategic partnerships and evidence-based development programs.

Vision, Mission & Values

Mission, Vision & Values

The principles and purpose that guide TL4A's work.

🎯

Mission Statement

To improve the quality of life of vulnerable and underserved populations by promoting access to quality education, healthcare, protection of human rights, and sustainable economic opportunities through advocacy, community-based interventions, capacity building, strategic partnerships, and evidence-based development programs

🌍

Vision Statement

TL4A envisages an improved qualitative life for all, unhindered rights in respect to education, health, and protection, enabling economic opportunity and sustainability.

🤝

Our Values

Inclusion, dignity, accountability, community participation, equality, sustainability and partnership.

Organizational Capacity

Experience, people and commitment

15+

Our Executive Director is a lawyer with over fifteen years’ experience in implementation of the Orphans and Vulnerable Children (OVC) program in Nigeria.

She has a great deal of experience in working with women and children over the years. The organisation is made up of volunteers and four staff including a qualified accountant.
